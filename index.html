Separately available, not pre-planned: OCC may extend the duty at the planning stage by up to 1h00 with crew agreement, no SM/NPFO sign-off needed (7.5.13). Minimum Rest Period

Standard minimum rest before an FDP. OMA 7.5.12

PIC's discretion to reduce rest further (OMA 7.5.15 — rewritten in this revision)

This is no longer a simple "not below 9 hours" rule. Before a PIC reduces rest below the 7.5.12 minimum, a documented fatigue risk assessment covering all crew is required, monitored by the NPFO and NPGO. It considers: length of preceding duty, current/perceived fatigue, frequency of recent extensions/reductions, acclimatisation status, impact of the time-of-day change, any additional duties before the next FDP, transport time to accommodation, and the length/complexity of the following FDP.

Reminder: the 60-minute minimum reporting time is part of the FDP and can never be reduced to accommodate a rest reduction, regardless of this floor.
Reporting timing (7.5.16): an FTL exceedance report goes in immediately after the flight. A reduction-of-rest report must go in before the next flight — the one following the reduced rest.

Quick lookup — definitions and clause map from OMA Section 7.5.

What changed vs. the 2021 edition (Issue 1/Rev 11)
  • Rest accommodation floor: 9h → 8h (7.5.12, 7.5.15)
  • 1-hour rest reduction trigger: previously only when earned rest was exactly 10h; now applies whenever earned rest is at least 10h
  • 7.5.15 PIC discretion to reduce rest: rewritten from a flat 9h floor into a formal, documented 11-factor fatigue risk assessment, monitored by NPFO/NPGO; floor is now 8h accommodation + actual transport time
  • 7.5.16 reporting timing made explicit: exceedance reports immediately after the flight; rest-reduction reports before the next flight
  • 7.5.9.1 long-range table: a sector over 11h when not acclimatised was previously "Not Applicable" — now permitted, counted as 3 sectors
Everything else — Table A/B values, split duty, in-flight relief, the 65/105/190/2000-hour duty limits, 100h/900h flight-time limits, and the days-off pattern — is unchanged.

Acclimatised
3 consecutive local nights on the ground in a time zone ≤2h wide with uninterrupted sleep. Stays acclimatised until a duty finishes somewhere local time differs by more than 2h from departure.
Local night
8 hours falling between 2200 and 0800 local time.
Days off
A single day off = 2 local nights, minimum 34 hours. Consecutive days off add a further local night each.
Sector
From an aircraft first moving under its own power to coming to rest after landing, at the parking position.
Split duty
An FDP of two or more sectors separated by less than a minimum rest period.
Suitable accommodation
A well-furnished bedroom with control over noise, ventilation, light, and temperature.
